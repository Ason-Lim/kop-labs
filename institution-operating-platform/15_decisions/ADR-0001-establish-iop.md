# ADR-0001 — Establish Institution Operating Platform

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-07-31

## Context

KOP Labs requires a platform layer that converts Institution Engineering principles and the Institution Operating System into reusable operational capabilities and domain platforms.

## Decision

Create the Institution Operating Platform (IOP) as an independent project and repository.

## Consequences

- IOP becomes the common execution platform beneath domain reference platforms.
- Domain-specific logic remains outside the IOP Core.
- Governance documents and architecture decisions are maintained as first-class artifacts.
- Canonical, Korean, and English representations are maintained together.
