# IOP-CHARTER-2026-001

## Institution Operating Platform Project Charter

- Document Status: DRAFT
- Version: 0.1
- Publisher: KOP Labs
- Governing Reference: IE Foundation Charter
- Project: Institution Operating Platform

## 1. Purpose

The Institution Operating Platform (IOP) establishes an execution foundation for applying Institution Engineering principles and the common operating model of the Institution Operating System (IOS) to institutions, companies, platforms, and public or industrial domains.

## 2. Official Motto

> Build Institutions, Not Just Software.

> We build institutions, not merely software.

## 3. Foundation Statement

> Software enables institutions. Institution Engineering enables the future of institutions.

## 4. Scope

- Common institutional operating capabilities
- Governance and decision-making
- Knowledge and documentation management
- Human–AI collaboration and automation
- Data and integration foundations
- Security, audit, and operations
- Domain reference platforms

## 5. Out of Scope

During the initial phase, domain-specific business functions shall not be directly coupled to the IOP Core. Such functions shall be managed as independent reference platforms or domain modules.

## 6. Governance

- Documentation Governance: 00_0 Foundation Documentation Council
- Architecture Governance: 00_1 Foundation Architecture Council
- Official Record: GitHub Repository of Record
- Change Records: ADRs, RFCs, and Release Notes

## 7. Reporting Principle

Verified facts, assumptions, and unverified items shall be clearly distinguished.
