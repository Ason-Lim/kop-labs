# Institution Operating Platform Established

**Document ID:** IEFM-0002
**Status:** Official Foundation Milestone
**Date:** 2026-08-03

## Purpose
Establish the Institution Operating Platform (IOP) as the canonical platform architecture.

## Ratified Decisions
1. IE Foundation is the canonical foundation.
2. IOS is the common operating model.
3. IOP is the canonical platform architecture.
4. Commerce AI Platform becomes Reference Implementation #1.
5. Future platforms inherit the Foundation.
6. KO/EN dual documentation.
7. GitHub is the Official Repository of Record.

## Motto
Build Institutions, Not Just Software.
우리는 소프트웨어가 아니라 Institution을 만든다.
