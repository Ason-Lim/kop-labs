# Research Experiments

This directory contains official Operational Research Experiments (EXP).

Experiments are planned research activities that generate operational evidence.

Research Lifecycle

RQ
↓
Hypothesis
↓
Experiment (EXP)
↓
Operational Evidence (OE)
↓
Validation (VAL)
↓
Reference Model (RM)

Every experiment shall be traceable to its related research question,
hypothesis, evidence, and validation artifacts.
