# Research Program

**Document ID:** RP-2026-002  
**Title:** Cross-Domain Generalization Program  
**Version:** Official v1.0  
**Status:** Active Research Program  
**Owner:** Institution Design Studio  
**Parent Program:** RP-2026-001 — Institutional Knowledge Production  
**Program Type:** Multi-Sprint Operational Research Program

## 1. Purpose

The Cross-Domain Generalization Program evaluates whether Institution
Engineering remains valid when repeatedly applied across heterogeneous
knowledge domains.

RP-2026-001 established the institutional knowledge-production system.
RP-2026-002 tests its generality, reproducibility, and scalability through real
product development and operational evidence.

## 2. Background

SPRINT-2026-004 completed the first formally registered Operational Research
Cycle using the Duck domain in the Commerce AI Platform.

That cycle demonstrated that engineering verification can be represented as a
traceable sequence of Experiment, Operational Evidence, Validation, and
Reference Model revision.

However, Duck was a previously developed domain. The result established
reproducibility, but not cross-domain generality.

## 3. Program Objective

Determine whether Institution Engineering can operate consistently across
multiple knowledge domains without requiring fundamental methodological
redesign.

The program shall evaluate:

- cross-domain applicability;
- repeatability;
- evidence quality;
- methodological stability;
- product–research interaction;
- Reference Model evolution.

## 4. Overarching Research Question

> Can Institution Engineering be generalized across heterogeneous knowledge
> domains through repeated Operational Research Cycles?

## 5. Initial Domain Sequence

| Sequence | Domain | Research Role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Goat | First newly completed cross-domain case |
| 2 | Cheese | Knowledge-structure expansion |
| 3 | Coffee | Registry and parser generalization |
| 4 | Wine | Complex knowledge-domain validation |

Additional domains may be introduced through program review.

## 6. Research Lifecycle

Each domain shall execute:

```text
Research Question
        ↓
Hypothesis
        ↓
Architecture Review
        ↓
Development
        ↓
Experiment
        ↓
Operational Evidence
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Reference Model Revision
```

A lifecycle may be revised only when new operational evidence demonstrates that
the current model is insufficient.

## 7. Evidence Categories

### Historical Reproduction Evidence

Evidence reproduced from previously completed development.

Purpose:

- verify reproducibility;
- reconstruct traceability;
- validate execution infrastructure.

### Novel Development Evidence

Evidence generated while completing an unfinished or new domain.

Purpose:

- validate generalization;
- expose methodological limits;
- improve both product and research framework.

Goat, Cheese, Coffee, and Wine are intended to generate novel development
evidence.

## 8. Program Success Criteria

The program is successful when:

1. multiple heterogeneous domains complete Operational Research Cycles;
2. experiment protocols remain reproducible;
3. evidence can be independently traced to execution;
4. validation produces meaningful product or methodology decisions;
5. Reference Models stabilize through incremental revision;
6. no domain is declared successful without evidence;
7. cross-domain findings can be distinguished from domain-specific findings.

## 11. Program Principles

> Every Foundation concept shall eventually produce operational evidence.

> Every product improvement shall be considered a potential research
> opportunity.

> Institution Engineering shall improve through repeated operational
> application rather than isolated theoretical refinement.

> Every completed Operational Research Cycle shall improve the product, the
> methodology, or both.

> Evidence shall supersede planning assumptions.

## 12. Maturity-Based Operation

The program is governed by evidence maturity, not calendar deadlines alone.

```text
Static Evidence
        ↓
Controlled Test Evidence
        ↓
Runtime Integration Evidence
        ↓
Novel Development Evidence
        ↓
Repeated Cross-Domain Evidence
        ↓
Cross-Platform Evidence
```

Calendar ranges are planning estimates. Stage transitions require evidence.
