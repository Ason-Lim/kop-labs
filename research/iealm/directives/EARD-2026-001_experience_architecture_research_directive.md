# EARD-2026-001 — Commerce AI Experience Architecture Research Directive

**Version:** Research Draft v0.1  
**Status:** Architecture Research Directive  
**Prepared By:** Institution Design Studio  
**Architecture Need:** EANC-2026-001  
**Classification:** N3  
**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Purpose

This directive authorizes structured Architecture Research while prohibiting premature implementation.

Protected architecture includes Marketplace Core, Market Intelligence, Recommendation Engine, Food Knowledge, and the PACD-2026-001 governed baseline.

## Primary Questions

The research shall answer:

- What belongs inside Experience Architecture?
- What must remain outside it?
- Is an explicit Experience Application layer needed?
- Who owns Experience state?
- What is the canonical Experience Data Contract?
- Where should semantic adaptation occur?
- What should renderers be allowed to do?
- What is the effective Recommendation public contract?
- How should Experience access infrastructure?
- Should Admin Experience be separate?
- Who owns explainability transformation?
- What evidence is necessary for Experience verification?

## Alternatives Requirement

At least three materially distinct alternatives shall be evaluated:

```text
Alternative A
Minimal Governance / Current Structure Preservation

Alternative B
Experience Application Boundary Introduction

Alternative C
Canonical Experience Contract + Strong Layer Separation
```
