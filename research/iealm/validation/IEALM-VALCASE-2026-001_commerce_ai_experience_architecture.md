## Purpose

This document establishes the first prospective operational validation case for IEALM.

Selected subject:

> Establishment of a Governed Commerce AI Experience Architecture

Existing Experience Design shall be treated as inherited architectural evidence, not discarded as legacy material.

Observed user-facing capabilities include search interaction, intent interpretation, recommendation presentation, Hero recommendation, explainability, product comparison, market information, personalization, interaction logging, click tracking, revisit recommendation, and purchase-oriented CTA.

## Research Finding

**IEALM-F-006**

Commerce AI Generator possesses substantial Experience Design and implementation, but current evidence does not demonstrate a unified canonical Experience Architecture lifecycle with explicit authority, contracts, evidence requirements, independent verification, decision, closure, and baseline inheritance.

**Status:** SUPPORTED
