# Hypothesis

**Document ID:** H-2026-002  
**Title:** Goat Domain Generalization Hypothesis  
**Version:** Official v1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Program:** RP-2026-002  
**Research Question:** RQ-2026-002  
**Owner:** Institution Design Studio

## 1. Hypothesis

> The Institution Engineering Operational Research Framework can guide the
> completion and validation of the Goat knowledge domain while producing
> reproducible novel evidence without requiring fundamental methodological
> redesign.

## 2. Predicted Observations

If supported:

- existing architectural responsibilities remain applicable;
- Goat-specific knowledge can be represented without breaking common contracts;
- development activities generate traceable evidence;
- failures can be classified without invalidating the research cycle;
- the same RQ → H → AR → Development → EXP → OE → VAL → RM lifecycle remains
  usable;
- RM changes are incremental rather than foundational.

## 3. Falsifying Observations

The hypothesis may be weakened or rejected if:

- Goat cannot fit the current domain architecture without major contract changes;
- evidence cannot be traced to development and execution;
- validation cannot distinguish product issues from methodology issues;
- the lifecycle requires fundamental restructuring;
- Goat-specific success cannot be separated from cross-domain conclusions.

## 4. Evidence Requirements

Required evidence includes:

- repository baseline;
- implementation delta;
- test commands and outputs;
- provider registration and selection;
- representative Goat product analysis;
- relevant regression results;
- limitations and counterexamples.

## 5. Decision States

- Supported
- Partially Supported
- Inconclusive
- Rejected

No decision shall be issued before OE-2026-003.
