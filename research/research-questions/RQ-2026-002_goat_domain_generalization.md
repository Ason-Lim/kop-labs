# Research Question

**Document ID:** RQ-2026-002  
**Title:** Goat Domain Generalization  
**Version:** Official v1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Program:** RP-2026-002  
**Owner:** Institution Design Studio  
**Primary Platform:** Commerce AI Platform

## 1. Background

Duck demonstrated reproducible execution of an Operational Research Cycle, but
it was a previously developed domain.

Goat is the first unfinished domain selected to generate novel development
evidence under RP-2026-002.

## 2. Research Problem

Current evidence does not establish whether Institution Engineering can guide
development and validation of a different domain while preserving traceability,
evidence quality, and methodological stability.

## 3. Research Question

> Can Institution Engineering produce reproducible operational evidence and
> institutional learning while completing and validating the Goat knowledge
> domain?

## 4. Scope

Included:

- Goat Type Registry;
- Goat Breed Registry;
- Goat Cut Registry;
- Registry YAML;
- Parser and parser models;
- Attributes;
- Rules;
- Scoring;
- Provider;
- provider registration;
- focused tests;
- integration and relevant regression tests.

Excluded unless separately authorized:

- unrelated domain changes;
- marketplace redesign;
- UI redesign;
- cross-platform implementation;
- broad common-framework refactoring.

## 6. Answer Criteria

RQ-2026-002 may be closed only when:

1. the initial repository state is recorded;
2. incomplete implementation is distinguished from completed implementation;
3. authorized Goat development is completed or explicitly limited;
4. the experiment is executed;
5. evidence includes successes, failures, and environment conditions;
6. validation evaluates both product and methodology;
7. cross-domain implications are recorded.

## 7. Expected Contribution

The primary contribution is not Goat functionality alone.

The research contribution is evidence about whether Institution Engineering can
generalize from a reproduced Duck case to a newly completed Goat case.
