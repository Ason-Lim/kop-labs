---

# 8. Regulatory Gate Requirement

The review confirms:

```text
REGULATORY GATE:
REQUIRED
```

Cross-border recommendations may depend on customs, import restrictions,
duties, taxes, shipping eligibility, destination-country rules, or other
regulatory evidence.

The architecture must therefore preserve an explicit regulatory
decision boundary rather than silently treating regulatory uncertainty
as ordinary recommendation data.

---

# 9. Evidence Provenance Requirement

The review confirms:

```text
EVIDENCE PROVENANCE:
REQUIRED
```

Cross-border intelligence may depend on evidence originating from
multiple external or internal sources.

Subsequent architecture work must preserve sufficient provenance to
distinguish:

```text
observed evidence
derived evidence
estimated evidence
unavailable evidence
unknown evidence
```

Evidence uncertainty SHALL NOT be silently converted into certainty.

---

# 10. Unknown-State Preservation

The review confirms:

```text
UNKNOWN STATE:
REQUIRED
```

The architecture must preserve unknown or unavailable states where
reliable evidence does not exist.

Missing:

```text
price
shipping cost
duty
tax
regulatory eligibility
availability
delivery estimate
```

must not automatically be interpreted as zero, false, eligible,
available, or otherwise known.

---

# 11. Transaction Execution Boundary

The review confirms:

```text
TRANSACTION EXECUTION:
OUT OF SCOPE
```

The current architecture direction concerns intelligence,
recommendation, comparison, evidence, and decision support.

This review does not authorize Commerce AI to become a payment,
purchasing, customs brokerage, fulfillment, or transaction-execution
system.

Any future expansion into transaction execution requires a separate
architecture decision.

# 20. No Silent Certainty Rule

Cross-border decision support inherently depends on evidence that may
be incomplete, delayed, estimated, jurisdiction-dependent, or
source-dependent.

Therefore subsequent architecture SHALL NOT silently transform
uncertain evidence into deterministic facts.

The following distinctions must remain representable:

```text
known
estimated
derived
unavailable
unknown
not applicable
```

This rule is part of the approved architecture direction.
