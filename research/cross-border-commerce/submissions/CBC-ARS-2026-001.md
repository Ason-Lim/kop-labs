# 10. Regulatory Gate Requirement

The proposal requires:

```text
REGULATORY GATE:
REQUIRED
```

Cross-Border Commerce recommendations may depend on jurisdiction,
customs, import/export constraints, restricted-item conditions,
tax/duty applicability, or other regulatory evidence.

The architecture must therefore prevent unverified regulatory assumptions
from silently becoming canonical recommendation facts.

---

# 11. Evidence Provenance Requirement

The proposal requires:

```text
EVIDENCE PROVENANCE:
REQUIRED
```

Cross-border recommendation evidence may originate from heterogeneous
sources with different freshness, authority, coverage, and reliability.

The architecture should preserve evidence source identity and avoid
collapsing derived estimates into authoritative facts.

---

# 12. Unknown-State Requirement

The proposal requires:

```text
UNKNOWN STATE:
REQUIRED
```

Unknown or unavailable cross-border information must remain representable
as unknown.

The architecture must not force missing regulatory, shipping, landed-cost,
availability, or marketplace evidence into false certainty.

# 17. Architecture Risks

The research submission identifies several architecture risks requiring
00_1 review.

## 17.1 Semantic Duplication

Cross-border implementation could duplicate existing Recommendation,
Marketplace, Product Identity, or Market Intelligence responsibility.

## 17.2 Regulatory Overreach

The system could present incomplete regulatory evidence as authoritative.

## 17.3 Landed-Cost False Precision

Shipping, duty, tax, forwarding, and fee estimates may be incomplete or
time-sensitive.

## 17.4 Evidence Collapse

Evidence from marketplaces, logistics providers, government sources, and
derived estimation could lose provenance.

## 17.5 Unknown-State Loss

Unavailable evidence could be coerced into default values that imply
certainty.

## 17.6 Scope Expansion

The architecture could drift from intelligence into transaction
execution without authorization.

These risks require explicit architecture controls.
