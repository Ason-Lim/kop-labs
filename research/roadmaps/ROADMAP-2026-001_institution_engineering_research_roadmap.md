# Research Roadmap

**Document ID:** ROADMAP-2026-001  
**Title:** Institution Engineering Research Roadmap  
**Version:** Official v1.0  
**Status:** Active Planning Baseline  
**Owner:** Institution Design Studio  
**Governance:** 00_0 / 00_2 review as applicable

## 1. Purpose

Define the long-term empirical evolution of Institution Engineering through
evidence-based maturity stages.

This roadmap is not a fixed delivery promise. Calendar ranges are planning
estimates; stage completion depends on evidence and governance.

## 2. Roadmap Principle

> Institution Engineering advances by demonstrated maturity, not by elapsed
> time alone.

## 5. Future Stages

### Stage 5 — Commerce AI Platform Generalization

Validate Recommendation, Marketplace, Knowledge Engine, Market Intelligence,
Product Intelligence, and user-feedback loops as a coherent living research
platform.

### Stage 6 — Cross-Platform Generalization

Apply the framework to Company Operating Platform and compare evidence with
Commerce AI.

### Stage 7 — Institution Runtime Validation

Derive and validate IOS runtime capabilities from repeated platform evidence.

### Stage 8 — Institution Platform Validation

Validate IOP shared APIs, SDK, security, AI framework, UI, and shared services.

### Stage 9 — Institution Ecosystem Validation

Extend to Healthcare, Education, and Public Sector platforms.

### Stage 10 — Foundation Maturation

Evaluate stable theories and principles for FTR and FP progression after
cross-domain, cross-platform, and independent validation.

## 7. Stage-Gate Criteria

A stage closes only when:

- required operational cases are complete;
- evidence is reproducible;
- counterexamples are recorded;
- validation is issued;
- Reference Model impact is determined;
- governance records the transition.
