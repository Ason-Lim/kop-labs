# 1. Milestone Declaration

KOP Labs recognizes the official registration of **JDM-2026-001** as a major research and governance milestone.

This milestone establishes the first formal case in which the emerging Institution Engineering Research Methodology was applied to one of its own methodological discoveries.

The milestone does not adopt Methodological Reflexivity as a Foundation Principle.

It establishes that Methodological Reflexivity has entered the formal research and governance process as a Foundation Principle Candidate.

---

# 2. Institutional Principle Demonstrated

The following principle was demonstrated through institutional practice:

> No idea becomes Foundation merely because it appears persuasive, originates from an authoritative participant, or receives early institutional support.

Conversely:

> No idea shall be rejected merely because of its origin, authorship, or institutional position.

All Foundation candidates shall mature through the same research methodology and governance process before institutional adoption.

# 4. Institutional Roles Confirmed

## Institution Design Studio

The Institution Design Studio:

* observes institutional phenomena;
* records discoveries;
* develops reference models;
* collects and evaluates evidence;
* performs validation;
* submits Foundation candidates for independent review.

The Institution Design Studio does not possess the authority to declare or adopt Foundation.

## 00_0 Master Document Governance

00_0 reviews:

* document identity;
* documentation governance;
* procedural consistency;
* traceability;
* canonicality;
* institutional registration.

## 00_2 IE Foundation

00_2 reviews:

* Foundation relevance;
* theoretical coherence;
* principle classification;
* evidence requirements;
* eligibility for Foundation maturation.

## Project Owner

The Project Owner retains final approval authority for Foundation adoption.
